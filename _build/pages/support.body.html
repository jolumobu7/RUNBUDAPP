Support

Email support@myrunbud.com. We reply within 24 to 48 hours.

Report a bug

Email us with:

  • Your phone model and its iOS or Android version
  • The RunBud version (Profile, at the bottom)
  • What you did and what happened
  • A screenshot, if you can

Report a person or a message

In the app, long-press the message or tap the flag on a forum post, then choose Report. The RunBud team reviews reports within 24 hours. If someone is in danger, call 911 first. Read our child safety standards and community guidelines.

Common questions

I forgot my password

Go to Sign in, enter your email and choose Forgot your password. The app has the same option.

How do I join my team?

Ask your coach for the team's 6-letter code. In the app, open Team, then Join a team.

My watch runs aren't showing up

In the app, open Log, then Watch, and connect Apple Health or Health Connect. Make sure your watch app syncs workouts to Health.

How do I delete my account?

In the app go to Profile, then Delete account, or delete it on this site.

Is RunBud medical advice?

No. RunBud shares general training information. For pain, injury or health concerns, talk to a doctor, athletic trainer or physical therapist.
