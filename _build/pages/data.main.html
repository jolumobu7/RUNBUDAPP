Data we collect

Updated November 19, 2026. This page lists every kind of data RunBud handles. It goes with our privacy policy.

The short version

  • Most of your training data stays on your phone today.
  • Your account (email and sign-in) is stored with Google Firebase.
  • Apple Health data is read only with your permission, one switch per metric, and is never written back.
  • No third-party AI service receives your data. No advertising trackers.
  • Items marked Coming are not collected yet. We'll update this page and ask in the app first.

On this website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ad trackers. The site saves one setting in your browser (light or dark theme).
  • Hosting: GitHub Pages serves the site. Like any web host, it receives your IP address and browser details to deliver pages.
  • Fonts: fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when loading them.
  • Coach demo: everything in the demo is sample data and stays in your browser tab. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
  • Email: if you email us, we receive what you send.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't use advertising trackers or build advertising profiles.
  • We don't send your data to third-party AI services.
  • We don't read from Apple Health anything you haven't switched on, and we never write to it.

Your controls

  • Turn each Apple Health metric on or off in the app, or remove RunBud's access in the Health app.
  • Edit or delete any entry in your training log.
  • Delete your account and data.
  • Ask for a copy of your data: support@myrunbud.com.
