What I'm building

RunBud

Founder. Live on the App Store, version 2.0 in beta

A free training app for track and field. Version 2.0 adds RunBud Coach, which builds and adjusts a training plan from an athlete's PRs, history and daily check-ins, plus a training log, teams, coach and school tools, and Android.

  • Took the app through Apple's review process, including rejections, fixes and resubmissions
  • Firebase Authentication with account deletion, in-app purchases with StoreKit, Apple Health permissions
  • Removed a third-party AI chat feature to keep user data out of outside services, and rewrote the privacy policy to match
  • Rebuilt the app in React Native and Expo for iPhone, iPad and Android, with home screen widgets and Apple Health and Health Connect import
  • Wrote and tested Firestore security rules for schools, coaches and athletes, where athletes control what each team can see and school administrators can review messages
  • Built and deployed this website on GitHub Pages with a custom domain and HTTPS
