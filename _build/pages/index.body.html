RunBud Coach, for when you're on your own

For summers, off-seasons, or athletes without a coach. It isn't a chatbot guessing. It uses the same principles coaches use, applied to your numbers, and it shows its work.

  • Your PRsBest marks in any event, converted so a 200 sets your 400 paces.
  • Your historyFade on the last reps or miss a day, and the next weeks change.
  • How you feelA ten-second check-in. Sick, sore or short on sleep makes a hard day easier.
  • Your weekPractice, class, work, other sports. Hard days never land back to back.

Questions

Something else? Email support@myrunbud.com. We reply within 24 to 48 hours.

Is RunBud free?

Yes. RunBud and RunBud Coach are free, with optional donations in the app.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone, on the App Store. RunBud 2.0 for iPhone and Android is in beta testing now.

Can I make an account here and use it in the app?

Yes. It's the same account. Create one here, then sign in on your phone.

Is RunBud trying to replace coaches?

No. When a coach posts a workout, that is the athlete's plan, and RunBud Coach won't plan over it. RunBud Coach is for athletes training on their own. For coaches, RunBud is the place to post workouts and see how every athlete is doing.

I'm a parent. Is RunBud safe for my athlete?

RunBud is for ages 13 and up. Team messages can be reviewed by the school, any chat with a coach in it is visible to every coach on the team, photos can't be sent, and anyone can report a message. Read our child safety standards.

Does it cost anything for a team or school?

Team, coach and school features are free while RunBud 2.0 is in beta. Email support@myrunbud.com to bring your team on.

Can athletic trainers and nutritionists use it?

Yes. They join a team with a staff code, and each athlete chooses what they can see.

Does RunBud work for my event?

RunBud Coach plans for sprints, hurdles, middle distance, distance, jumps, throws and multi-events.

How do I delete my account?

In the app go to Profile, then Delete account, or delete it on this site.

Is this medical advice?

No. RunBud shares general training information. For pain, injury or health concerns, talk to a doctor, athletic trainer or physical therapist.
