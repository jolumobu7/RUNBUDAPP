Delete your account

You can delete your RunBud account at any time, in the app or right here. You don't need to reinstall the app.

In the app

  1. Open RunBud and go to Profile (in older versions, Settings).
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If the app asks you to sign in again first, that's a security check. Sign out, sign back in, and try again.

On this page

Sign in to delete

Signed in as .

If you signed up with Apple or Google instead of a password, delete your account in the app or by email.

By email

If you can't get into the app, email support@myrunbud.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my RunBud account." We'll confirm within 48 hours and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email, password, phone number if used).
  • Your training backup on RunBud's servers: sessions, results and check-ins.
  • Your team memberships and everything you shared with coaches, trainers and nutritionists.
  • Your forum profile, posts and replies, and any badge application.
  • Everything RunBud saved on your phone, when you delete in the app (training log, imported watch data, profile and settings).

What may remain

  • Team messages you already sent. They're kept as a safety record for the school or club that runs the team, as the app explains before you message.
  • Purchase records kept by Apple or Google under their policies.
  • Emails you sent us, unless you ask us to delete them.
  • Backups and security logs, for a short time before they're removed.
  • Your Apple Health or Health Connect data, which belongs to those apps. RunBud never changes it.
