Delete your account

You can delete your RunBud account at any time.

In the app

  1. Open RunBud and go to Profile (in older versions, Settings).
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If the app asks you to sign in again first, that's a security check. Sign out, sign back in, and try again.

By email

If you can't get into the app, email support@myrunbud.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my RunBud account." We'll confirm within 48 hours and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email, password, phone number if used).
  • Everything RunBud saved on your phone: training log, results, check-ins, imported watch data, profile and settings.
  • When team features launch: anything synced to RunBud's servers and your team memberships.

What may remain

  • Purchase records kept by Apple under Apple's policy.
  • Emails you sent us, unless you ask us to delete them.
  • Backups and security logs, for a short time before they're removed.
  • Your Apple Health data, which belongs to the Health app. RunBud never changes it.
