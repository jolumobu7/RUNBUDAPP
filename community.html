Community guidelines

These apply to team features and messaging, launching with the spring 2027 coach pilot.

The basics

  • Be respectful. Disagree about training, not about people.
  • Keep it about training, school and team logistics.
  • No harassment, threats, hate, sexual content, or sharing anyone's private information.
  • Don't share someone's training or health data outside the team without their permission.

Coaches and athletes under 18

  • Coach and athlete conversations happen in RunBud's monitored chats, which the school or club's admins can review.
  • Coaches should not move conversations with minors to private apps, texts or social media.
  • Prefer group and event-group chats over one-on-one messages.
  • Follow your school's or club's safeguarding policy. It applies on RunBud too.

Reporting

Every message has a Report button. Reports go to your school or club admins and, for serious abuse, to RunBud. You can also block someone. If anyone is in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number first.

What happens when rules are broken

Admins can remove messages and members. RunBud can suspend or close accounts and, where the law requires, report to authorities.
