Set up your team in five minutes

Everything starts in the app. After that, the hub works on any screen.

  1. Create a teamIn the app, open Team, then Create a team. You become the head coach.
  2. Share two codesAn athlete code for athletes and a staff code for assistant coaches, athletic trainers and nutritionists.
  3. Athletes joinEach athlete enters the code, picks their events and chooses what the team can see.
  4. Post the first workoutPick the day, the group and when athletes see it. It lands on their Today screen.
  5. Open the hubSign in at myrunbud.com/hub.html with the same account to see the whole roster.

Roles on a team

Everyone on a team has one role. The head coach can change staff roles and remove people. Nobody can change their own role.

Head coach and coaches

Post and edit workouts, see the roster, read what athletes share with coaches, message the team, and create group chats.

Athletic trainers

Join with the staff code. By default they see sessions and check-ins, so pain and recovery flags reach them early.

Nutritionists

Join with the staff code. They see nothing until an athlete chooses to share with them.

Athletes

Follow the team plan, log training and decide, role by role, what the team can see. They can leave a team at any time.

Roster

Every athlete with their event, today's check-in, last session and this week's load. Athletes who need attention are listed first. In the app you can change a staff member's role or remove someone.

Athlete detail

Their week, their load against the last four weeks, their last check-in, and the sessions and results they share.

Need attention

Pain, sickness and poor sleep from the morning check-in, so you can change a session before practice.

Posting workouts

Write the day the way you'd write it on the board: warm-up, main set, weight room, cool-down.

  • Who sees it: the whole team or one event group, like sprints or distance
  • When they see it: right away, the night before at 8 PM, that morning at 6 AM, or that day at noon
  • Details: title, parts, planned minutes, planned effort and notes for athletes
  • Edits: change or delete a workout any time, and athletes' Today screens update
  • Your plan comes first: on a day you post, RunBud Coach won't plan anything else for your athletes

Team messages, built to be safe

Many athletes are 13 to 17. Messaging is designed so no conversation with an adult is hidden.

Kinds of chats

  • Team chat for everyone on the team
  • Group chats that coaches create, always visible to staff
  • Direct messages between two people on the team

Rules that can't be switched off

  • Any chat with a coach or staff member in it is visible to every staff member
  • Messages can't be edited or deleted
  • No photos or videos, and phone numbers and emails are blocked
  • Anyone can report a message or block a person
  • Reports reach the RunBud team, who review them within 24 hours

Coach questions

Will RunBud change my workouts?

No. On days you post, that's the plan. RunBud Coach only plans for athletes training on their own, or on days without a team workout.

Can I coach more than one team?

Yes. Each team has its own codes, roster and chats. Athletes can be on more than one team too, like a school team and a club.

What if an athlete doesn't share anything?

You'll still see them on the roster and they'll still get your workouts. You just won't see their logs until they share.

Does it cost anything?

Team, coach and school features are free while RunBud 2.0 is in beta.

Where's the data stored?

Accounts and team data are stored with Google Firebase and protected by access rules we test. See the privacy policy.
