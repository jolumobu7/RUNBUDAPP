Privacy policy

Effective October 28, 2026. This replaces the policy dated September 29, 2025.

Who we are

RunBud is an iPhone app for track and field athletes. It is operated by Jesufifunmi Olugbenga, doing business as RunBud. In this policy, “we,” “our” and “us” mean RunBud. This policy covers the RunBud app and this website.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address and sign-in details, and your name if you provide it. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a Google service.
  • Workout and activity data: runs and workouts you record, such as time, distance and pace.
  • Wellness entries: hydration logs and any nutrition, sleep or recovery entries you choose to make.
  • Messages to us: emails you send for support, including anything you include in them.

Information collected from your device

  • Precise location (GPS): only while you use a feature that tracks a run, and only if you allow location access in iOS. You can turn it off at any time in iPhone Settings.
  • Basic technical information: app version, device model, iOS version and diagnostic or crash information, used to find and fix problems.

Purchases

Optional donations are handled by Apple through in-app purchase. Apple processes the payment. We never see your card details; we only learn that a purchase happened.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, save your workouts and entries, and show your history.
  • To give you training, hydration, sleep and recovery tools.
  • To keep the app working, secure and free of abuse.
  • To answer your support messages and tell you about important changes.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your data.

Who we share it with

  • Service providers that run the app for us. We use Google Firebase for sign-in and data storage. These providers may process your information only to provide their service to us.
  • Apple, for the App Store and in-app purchases, under Apple’s own privacy policy.
  • Stores you visit through the app. The Shop opens a store run by someone else. That store collects its own information under its own policy. Some Shop links may be affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission on a purchase. We receive information about the purchase, not your payment details.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it or to protect people’s safety.
  • A buyer or successor, if RunBud is ever sold or merged. Your information may transfer to the new owner, and this policy would continue to apply to it.

No third-party AI service receives your data. Earlier versions of RunBud included a chat assistant that sent your messages to an outside AI provider. That assistant has been removed.

Features in development

We are building a training log and tools for coaches. When they are released, they may collect training plans, workout logs, weight-room entries and daily check-ins. If you choose, they may also use sleep and heart-rate data from Apple Health, and let you share selected information with a coach you pick.

Before RunBud collects or shares any of this, we will update this policy and ask for your permission in the app. Each data source and feature will have its own on and off switch. We are building them so that no third-party AI service receives your health data.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account inside the app. Deleting it removes your account and the data stored with it. You can also email us and we will delete it for you. Some records, such as backups and legal or security logs, may remain for a short time before they are removed.

Security

We use Google Firebase, which encrypts data while it travels to and from the app and while it is stored. We limit who can access it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off location access or notifications at any time in iPhone Settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it by emailing us.
  • Depending on where you live, including California and other US states, and the EU or UK, you may have legal rights over your personal information. We will respond to a verified request as the law requires.

Health and medical information

RunBud shares general training and wellness information. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. For pain, injury or health concerns, talk to a doctor, athletic trainer or physical therapist. If you feel sharp pain, chest pain or dizziness, stop and get medical help.

Children and teens

RunBud is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it. If you are under 18, we encourage you to read this policy with a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top. For changes that matter, such as collecting a new kind of data, we will also tell you in the app and ask for your permission where needed.

Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: jolumobuhelp@gmail.com. We reply within 24 to 48 hours.
